Kinesis

Six living particle systems you can shape with your hands. For the full experience, enable your camera and conduct the field with gestures.

Press 1–6 to switch between the six particle systems. A hotkey guide sits in the corner — H hides it, Esc hides everything. Open “Advanced Controls for Nerds” on the right to tune every detail, and drag the camera preview’s corner to resize it.
